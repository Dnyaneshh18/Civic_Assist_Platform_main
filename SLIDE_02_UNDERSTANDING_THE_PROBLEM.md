# Slide 02: Understanding the Problem (DevVortex)
*MIT ACSC • ALANDI, PUNE | Kurukshetra 2.0 Hackfest*

---

### 🔲 TOP BOX: Explain the problem your team is solving, in your own words.

* **The "Black Hole" & "Ghost Closure" Crisis:** Citizens report daily infrastructure hazards (potholes, garbage dumps, dark streetlights), but complaints vanish into bureaucratic silos and are frequently marked *"Resolved"* without any actual on-site work done, destroying citizen trust.
* **Municipal Triage Overload:** Civic bodies are overwhelmed by thousands of unverified, duplicate, and spam submissions, wasting up to 40% of staff time manually sifting through noise instead of resolving real hazards.
* **Imprecise Location Data & Field Blindness:** Reports lack sub-meter coordinates (e.g. broad district labels like *"Pimpri-Chinchwad"* instead of *"MIT Alandi, Dehu - Alandi Road"*), leaving field inspection crews unable to pinpoint or reach the exact defect spot.

---

### 🔲 BOTTOM-LEFT BOX: Problem Analysis / Background

* **What is the current situation or existing problem?**
  * **Opaque Complaint Lifecycle:** Traditional portals (Swachhata, Aaple Sarkar, static web forms) lack live officer assignments, real-time timestamps, or verified photo proof of work.
  * **Manual, Unfiltered Processing:** Municipalities process complaints manually without AI computer vision, causing massive backlogs and delayed emergency response.
  * **Absence of Regional Geospatial Intelligence:** City administrations operate reactively without dynamic density heatmaps to spot systemic failure clusters across urban corridors like Mumbai and Pune.

* **Who is affected by it, and how significantly?**
  * **Daily Commuters & Pedestrians:** Face severe vehicle suspension damage, traffic congestion, and life-threatening road accidents from uninspected potholes and unlit streets.
  * **Local Communities & Campus Areas:** Suffer acute public health hazards and dengue/malaria risks from overflowing garbage piles and clogged drains near campuses and residential wards.

---

### 🔲 BOTTOM-RIGHT BOX: Who It Affects

* **Target users / stakeholders:**
  * **Urban Citizens & Campuses:** Students, commuters, and residents needing transparent, one-tap hazard reporting.
  * **Municipal Corporations (BMC, PCMC, PMC):** Ward officers and department heads (Solid Waste, Roads, Streetlights, Water) needing automated dispatch.
  * **Field Maintenance Crews:** On-ground repair teams requiring exact site navigation and mobile proof submission.

* **Scale of the problem (data, numbers, evidence):**
  * **30%–40%** of grievances submitted on legacy civic platforms are fake, duplicate, or spam photos.
  * **4,000+ road accidents annually** in India are directly linked to potholes and unaddressed road defects (MoRTH reports).
  * **Over 65% of citizens** express distrust in existing grievance portals due to "ghost closures" (tickets closed with zero physical repair).

* **Why existing approaches fall short:**
  * **No AI Fraud Prevention:** Cannot verify if uploaded photos match reported categories.
  * **No Mandatory Before/After Verification:** Departments self-close tickets with simple text notes.
  * **Zero Turn-by-Turn Field Navigation:** Crews are given text addresses with no GPS directions.
